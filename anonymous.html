The Full Stack Dentist
Scorecard

A dentist gets hired for one thing and measured on one thing: what they produce with their hands. That's the ceiling. The practices that grow extraordinarily aren't built by the best clinician in the building — they're built by the dentist who is round instead of spiky. Six domains, scored twice: once by the doctor, once by their lead clinician. The number isn't the point. The shape is — and so is the gap between how those two people see it.

Reference — how the five levels are defined

The Ladder

The same five levels apply to every domain. They aren't about how hard you work — they're about how far the thing you're good at travels beyond your own hands.

Level 3 is the plateau. A dentist who is a 3 across the board is a genuinely good associate — and will stay an associate. Every meaningful jump in a practice's growth comes from someone crossing 3 → 4 in a domain that isn't clinical.
